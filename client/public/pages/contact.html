Support Policy

FreeGridPaper is provided as a free, self-serve utility. Because the tool runs entirely in your browser and relies on your local printer handling PDF files correctly, we cannot provide individual technical support for printing issues, alignment corrections, or browser incompatibilities.

If your prints are scaling incorrectly, please ensure your printer settings are set to "Actual Size" or "100% Scale" rather than "Fit to Page."
