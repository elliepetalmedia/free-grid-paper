Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to FreeGridPaper.com, published by Ellie Petal Media.

1. Safe Client-Side Processing

This website processes your template data locally on your device. When you generate and download a PDF, the file is compiled strictly within your web browser's memory using JavaScript. No documents, customizations, or generated files are ever uploaded strictly to our servers. From a document generation standpoint, your data never leaves your computer.

2. Analytics & Cookies

We use industry-standard third-party analytics (Google Analytics) to understand website traffic, monitor site performance, and see which templates are most popular. This service may employ cookies and collect non-personally identifiable information such as browser type, operating system, and general aggregated demographic data to help us improve the site.

By using this website, you consent to our use of these analytics cookies. If we enable advertising in the future (such as Google AdSense), those services will also use cookies to serve personalized or non-personalized ads based on your browsing habits.

3. Local Storage

Your template settings (such as line width, color, and dot spacing) may be saved automatically to your browser's local storage so that your preferences are remembered the next time you visit. This data is stored entirely on your device and is not transmitted to us.

4. Contact

If you have any questions regarding this policy, please reach out to us at legal@freegridpaper.com.
